Owner-Operator Truck Finance

Owner-operators face unique challenges when accessing truck finance. Many lenders assess applications using tax returns that may not reflect the true earning capacity of an owner-operator due to legitimate tax minimisation strategies. Finance You has access to specialist lenders who:

  • Understand the transport industry and how owner-operators earn their income
  • Can assess applications based on BAS, bank statements and signed contracts rather than tax returns
  • Offer low-doc and no-doc options for established operators with a clean credit history
  • Provide competitive rates even for older or high-kilometre vehicles
  • Have dedicated transport finance specialists who understand the asset values

Low-Doc Truck Finance

Low-documentation (low-doc) truck finance is available for eligible owner-operators and transport businesses that may not have up-to-date financial statements. To be eligible for low-doc truck finance, you typically need:

  • An active ABN for at least 2 years
  • A self-declaration of income (signed by applicant)
  • Clean credit history (no defaults or recent bankruptcies)
  • Recent BAS statements showing business activity
  • Bank statements showing business turnover

Low-doc loans typically carry slightly higher rates than full-doc loans to reflect the reduced documentation. Finance You can advise on the best approach for your situation.

Fleet Finance

For transport companies operating or expanding a fleet, Finance You offers fleet finance solutions that can be structured to match your fleet replacement cycle and cash flow requirements. Fleet finance can include:

  • Bulk finance facilities for multiple vehicles at once
  • Staggered settlement to match vehicle delivery schedules
  • Mixed fleet finance (different vehicle types under one facility)
  • Refinancing of existing fleet to consolidate and reduce monthly costs

Truck Finance Structures

As with other equipment finance, truck finance can be structured as a chattel mortgage, hire purchase, or finance lease. For transport businesses, chattel mortgage is typically the most popular due to the immediate ownership and tax advantages for GST-registered businesses. Your Finance You broker will advise on the most suitable structure for your tax position and cash flow needs.
