3. Credit Services We Provide

Finance You provides "credit assistance" as defined under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (NCCP). This includes:

  • Credit assistance for consumer credit: Assisting consumers to apply for consumer credit contracts (including car loans, personal loans, and other credit regulated under the NCCP)
  • Equipment and asset finance: Arranging equipment finance, chattel mortgages, hire purchase agreements, and finance and operating leases for business purposes
  • Commercial finance: Arranging business loans, commercial property finance, invoice finance, trade finance, and other commercial lending products

Note: Commercial finance products (business loans, equipment finance for business use) are generally not regulated by the NCCP. However, Finance You applies responsible lending principles to all credit assistance, regardless of the regulatory category.

4. Our Lender Panel

Finance You is an independent broker and is not owned by or affiliated with any single lender. We work with a panel of over 40 Australian lenders, including:

  • Major banks: Australia's major authorised deposit-taking institutions (ADIs)
  • Non-bank lenders: Specialist non-bank finance companies offering competitive equipment and vehicle finance
  • Specialist equipment lenders: Lenders specialising in specific asset classes such as construction, agricultural, medical and technology equipment
  • Credit unions and mutual banks: Member-owned financial institutions offering competitive consumer lending
  • Online and fintech lenders: Digital-first lenders offering fast approvals for personal and business loans

A current list of our lender panel is available on request. Contact us at info@financeyou.com.au.

5. Fees and Charges We May Receive

Finance You may receive remuneration in the following ways:

5.1 Commissions from Lenders

In most cases, our broking service is provided to you at no direct cost. We receive a commission from the lender when your loan settles. Commission amounts vary by lender and product type, and typically range from:

  • Upfront commission: 0.5%–1.5% of the total loan amount
  • Trail commission: Some lenders pay ongoing trail commissions of 0.1%–0.25% p.a. of the outstanding loan balance for the life of the loan

We will disclose the specific commission amount we expect to receive before you commit to any loan arrangement.

5.2 Broker Fees

For complex commercial transactions, we may charge a broker fee directly to you. This fee will be disclosed upfront before any work is commenced and will be agreed in writing. Typical broker fees range from $500–$5,000 depending on the complexity of the transaction.

5.3 No Conflict of Interest

Finance You does not receive higher commissions from any lender in exchange for recommending that lender's products preferentially. Our recommendations are based on finding the most suitable and competitive product for each client's individual circumstances.

6. Your Responsibilities

To receive the best possible assistance, we ask that you:

  • Provide accurate and complete information about your financial situation, income, expenses, assets, and liabilities
  • Inform us of any changes to your financial circumstances during the application process
  • Read all loan documents carefully before signing
  • Ask us if you don't understand any aspect of your loan or the process
  • Only apply for credit that you genuinely require and can afford to repay

Providing false or misleading information in a credit application is a serious matter and may constitute fraud. It will also likely result in a loan being unsuitable for your circumstances.

7. Our Responsible Lending Obligations

For consumer credit products regulated by the NCCP, Finance You is required to:

  • Make reasonable enquiries about your financial situation, requirements, and objectives
  • Verify your financial information
  • Assess whether the proposed credit contract is "not unsuitable" for you
  • Not suggest, assist, or provide credit that is unsuitable for you

A credit contract is "unsuitable" if it does not meet your requirements and objectives, or if you would be unable to comply with your financial obligations under the contract, or could only comply with substantial hardship.

For commercial credit products outside the NCCP, Finance You applies similar principles of responsible lending as a matter of professional practice.

8. What to Do If You Have a Complaint

8.1 Internal Complaints Process

If you have a complaint about our services, we encourage you to contact us first:

  • Email: complaints@financeyou.com.au
  • Phone: 1300 989 282
  • Post: Complaints Officer, Finance You Pty Ltd, 114 Cambridge St, West Leederville WA 6007

We will acknowledge your complaint within 1 business day and aim to resolve it within 10 business days. If we need more time, we will keep you informed of progress.

8.2 External Dispute Resolution — AFCA

If you are not satisfied with our response, or if we fail to resolve your complaint within 45 days, you can refer your complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) — a free external dispute resolution scheme.

9. Credit Reporting

When you apply for credit, we and/or the lender may access information about you from a credit reporting body such as Equifax, Experian, or illion. This information may include details of your credit history, including previous applications for credit, defaults, and repayment history.

Each formal credit application results in an enquiry recorded on your credit file. Multiple enquiries in a short period can negatively affect your credit score. Finance You works to minimise unnecessary credit enquiries by assessing your eligibility before submitting formal applications.

You have the right to access and correct information held about you by a credit reporting body. Contact information for Australia's major credit reporting bodies is available on the ASIC MoneySmart website at moneysmart.gov.au.
