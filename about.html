About Finance You

We're an Australian finance broker on a mission to help every business and individual access the right finance — with expert guidance, genuine transparency, and a relentless focus on getting the best outcome for our clients.

Our Story

A Decade of Helping Australians Get Funded

Finance You was founded in 2015 with a simple belief: that every Australian — whether running a small business, buying a first car, or growing a fleet — deserves access to competitive finance options and expert advice.

Over the past decade, we've helped thousands of clients across Australia secure the right finance for their needs — from a first-time car buyer in regional Queensland to a construction company financing a $2 million earthmoving fleet.

Today, Finance You holds Australian Credit Licence 392533, issued by the Australian Securities and Investments Commission. We work with a panel of 40+ lenders — including major banks, non-bank lenders, specialist financiers, and credit unions — to ensure our clients always have access to the widest choice of competitive finance products.

Our team of experienced finance brokers has deep expertise across equipment finance, commercial lending, vehicle finance, and consumer lending — and we're committed to responsible, ethical broking in everything we do.

Our Regulatory Obligations

Finance You Pty Ltd holds Australian Credit Licence 392533, issued by the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC). We are bound by the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (NCCP) for all consumer credit products, and apply best-practice responsible lending standards to all commercial transactions.

Our obligations include:

  • Assessing that any recommended loan is "not unsuitable" for the consumer
  • Disclosing all commissions and conflicts of interest
  • Providing a Credit Guide to all credit assistance recipients
  • Maintaining an internal complaints process and membership of AFCA
  • Meeting ongoing training, competency and compliance requirements
