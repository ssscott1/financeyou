Types of Car Finance

🚗 Consumer Car Loan

For personal use vehicles. Fixed rate, fixed term (1–7 years), secured against the car. The most common finance option for individuals. NCCP-regulated, so you benefit from responsible lending protections.

🏢 Chattel Mortgage (Business)

For business-use vehicles. You own the car from day one. Claim GST input credits upfront, plus depreciation and interest as tax deductions. Best for GST-registered business owners.

📋 Finance Lease (Business)

Lender owns the car, you lease it. 100% of lease payments are tax deductible. Purchase for residual value at end of term or start a new lease. Good for businesses wanting off-balance-sheet treatment.

New Car Finance

New car loans typically attract the most competitive interest rates, as the vehicle provides strong security for the lender. Finance You can arrange new car finance through dealer introductions or independently — independent finance often delivers better rates than dealer-arranged finance.

Key points for new car finance:

  • Rates typically from 6.99% p.a. for prime borrowers
  • Terms from 1–7 years
  • Optional balloon payment to reduce monthly repayments
  • Comprehensive insurance required by most lenders
  • Finance can be arranged before you visit the dealership

Used Car Finance

Finance You can arrange loans for used vehicles from dealerships or private sellers. Used car loans typically carry slightly higher rates than new car loans due to the asset's age and reduced security value. Most lenders will finance vehicles up to 10–12 years old at the end of the loan term. For older vehicles or high-kilometre cars, specialist lenders may be required.

Electric Vehicle (EV) Finance

A balloon payment is a lump sum due at the end of your car loan term. Including a balloon reduces your monthly repayments during the loan but requires a larger payment at the end. You can pay it in cash, sell the car, trade in for a new vehicle, or refinance the balloon. Balloons are optional on consumer car loans and chattel mortgages, and are not permitted on loans regulated by the NCCP above a 50% residual.
