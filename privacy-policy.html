Privacy Policy

Finance You Pty Ltd — Last Updated: May 2025

1. Introduction

Finance You Pty Ltd ("Finance You", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of all individuals whose personal information we handle. We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) contained in that Act.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect your personal information. By using our website or engaging our services, you consent to our handling of your personal information in accordance with this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or wish to access or correct your personal information, please contact our Privacy Officer using the details in Section 13.

2. Information We Collect

The types of personal information we may collect include:

Identity Information

  • Full name, date of birth, gender
  • Government-issued identification (driver's licence number, passport number)
  • Residential and postal address (current and previous)

Contact Information

  • Phone number(s) (mobile and landline)
  • Email address

Financial Information

  • Income, employment details and payslips
  • Business financials (tax returns, financial statements, BAS)
  • Bank account details and bank statements
  • Existing debts, liabilities and expenses
  • Assets owned (property, vehicles, investments)
  • Credit history (obtained from credit reporting bodies with your consent)

Business Information

  • ABN, ACN, business name and trading history
  • Business address and contact details
  • Details of directors, shareholders, and guarantors

3. How We Collect Information

We collect personal information in the following ways:

  • Directly from you: via our website contact forms, telephone conversations, email, and application forms
  • From third parties: with your consent, from credit reporting bodies (e.g., Equifax, Experian, illion), lenders, accountants, and other financial professionals
  • From publicly available sources: such as ASIC's business register and the Personal Property Securities Register (PPSR)
  • Through our website: via cookies, analytics tools, and server logs (see Section 11)

4. Why We Collect Information

We collect and use personal information for the following primary purposes:

  • Assessing your financial circumstances and credit needs
  • Identifying and comparing suitable finance products from our lender panel
  • Preparing and submitting loan applications on your behalf
  • Communicating with lenders about your application
  • Complying with our obligations under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (NCCP) and our responsible lending obligations
  • Verifying your identity as required by anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing laws
  • Providing ongoing service and account management
  • Keeping records as required by law

5. Disclosure of Information

We may disclose your personal information to:

  • Lenders: Financial institutions and credit providers on our panel for the purpose of assessing and processing your loan application
  • Credit reporting bodies: To obtain and/or report credit information as part of the credit assessment process
  • Identity verification services: To verify your identity as required by law
  • Government agencies: Where required by law, such as ASIC, AUSTRAC, or tax authorities
  • Professional advisers: Such as solicitors, accountants, or valuers engaged in connection with your finance application
  • IT service providers: Cloud storage, CRM and communication platform providers who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations

We will not sell, rent, or disclose your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes without your express consent.

6. Credit-Related Personal Information

As a credit assistance provider, Finance You may handle "credit information" and "credit eligibility information" as defined under the Privacy Act. This includes:

  • Information about your credit applications and existing credit accounts
  • Your repayment history information
  • Default information
  • Credit scores and assessments

We handle credit information in accordance with the Privacy (Credit Reporting) Code 2014 (CR Code) as well as the APPs. We will only obtain credit reports and credit information with your consent, or where permitted by law.

7. Marketing Communications

We may use your contact details to send you information about Finance You's services that we think may be relevant to you. You may opt out of receiving marketing communications at any time by:

  • Clicking "unsubscribe" in any email we send you
  • Contacting us at privacy@financeyou.com.au
  • Calling us on 1300 989 282

Note that opting out of marketing communications does not affect our ability to send you transactional communications related to your loan application or existing accounts.

8. Data Security

Finance You takes reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. Our security measures include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted via our website
  • Secure, access-controlled document management systems
  • Restricted access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular staff training on privacy and data security
  • Secure disposal of physical documents containing personal information

Despite these measures, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. If you suspect a data breach affecting your information, please contact us immediately.

9. Access and Correction

Under the Privacy Act, you have the right to access personal information we hold about you, and to request that we correct it if it is inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date.

To make an access or correction request, please contact our Privacy Officer at:

  • Email: privacy@financeyou.com.au
  • Phone: 1300 989 282
  • Post: Privacy Officer, Finance You Pty Ltd, 114 Cambridge St, West Leederville WA 6007

We will respond to access and correction requests within 30 days. In most cases, we will provide access free of charge, though we may charge a reasonable fee for particularly complex requests.

10. Overseas Disclosure

Finance You may use cloud-based IT service providers that store data on servers located outside Australia (for example, in the United States or Singapore). We take reasonable steps to ensure that these providers comply with standards comparable to the APPs. By using our services, you consent to the potential transfer of your information to overseas recipients.

Where we disclose personal information to overseas recipients, we are still responsible for ensuring that information is handled in accordance with the APPs.

11. Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Improve your browsing experience and site functionality
  • Analyse website traffic and usage patterns (via Google Analytics)
  • Remember your preferences
  • Enable remarketing advertisements (if implemented)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect the functionality of our website. By continuing to use our website without disabling cookies, you consent to our use of cookies as described.

Google Analytics data is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Finance You uses anonymised IP addresses where available.

12. Complaints

If you believe we have handled your personal information in a way that contravenes the Privacy Act or the APPs, you can make a complaint by:

  • Email: privacy@financeyou.com.au
  • Phone: 1300 989 282
  • Post: Privacy Officer, Finance You Pty Ltd, 114 Cambridge St, West Leederville WA 6007

We will acknowledge your complaint within 5 business days and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may refer your complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC):

  • Website: www.oaic.gov.au
  • Phone: 1300 363 992
  • Post: GPO Box 5218, Sydney NSW 2001

For all privacy-related enquiries, access requests, correction requests, and complaints, please contact:

14. Changes to This Policy

Finance You may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

If we make significant changes that affect how we use your personal information, we will notify you directly by email (where we hold your email address) or by prominent notice on our website.
