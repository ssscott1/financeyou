Personal Loan Rates in Australia

Personal loan rates vary depending on the lender, loan type, amount, term and your credit profile. As a guide:

  • Secured personal loans: from approximately 9.99%–15% p.a. for prime borrowers
  • Unsecured personal loans: from approximately 12.99%–20% p.a. for prime borrowers
  • Specialist/bad credit: 18%–30%+ p.a. depending on circumstances

Finance You searches 40+ lenders to find the most competitive rate for your profile. Small differences in interest rate can make a significant difference to total repayments over the loan term — a 2% rate difference on a $30,000 loan over 5 years saves approximately $1,600 in interest.

Debt Consolidation Loans

Many Australians carry high-rate credit card debt (often 18%–22% p.a.) alongside multiple personal loans and buy-now-pay-later obligations. Personal Loan FAQ

A secured personal loan is backed by a physical asset — typically a car, boat, or other asset — which the lender can repossess if you default. This reduces the lender's risk and results in lower interest rates. An unsecured personal loan has no collateral requirement, which makes it accessible to more borrowers, but the rates are higher to compensate the lender for the increased risk.

Unsecured personal loan amounts typically range from $2,000 to $50,000, though some lenders go higher. Secured personal loans (particularly car loans) can range from $5,000 to $150,000 or more. The maximum you can borrow depends on your income, expenses, credit history, and existing debt obligations. Lenders are required to lend responsibly and will assess your capacity to repay without financial hardship.

Personal loan approval times vary by lender. Some online lenders can provide approval within hours and fund the same day for approved applications. Traditional bank lenders typically take 1–3 business days. Finance You works with lenders across the spectrum so we can match you with a lender whose speed of approval meets your needs.

There is no single credit score requirement, as different lenders have different criteria. Prime lenders typically want a good to excellent credit score, while specialist and non-bank lenders can accommodate a wider range of credit profiles including some adverse credit. Finance You will assess your credit profile before applying to lenders to identify the most suitable options and minimise the chance of rejection, which can further impact your credit file.

Debt consolidation can be worth it if the personal loan rate is significantly lower than your existing debts (particularly credit cards at 18%–22%), if it simplifies your repayments, and if you're committed to not accumulating new debt. It's important to compare the total cost over the full loan term, including any fees, not just the monthly repayment. Finance You brokers can run the numbers with you to confirm whether it makes financial sense.

Many personal loans allow extra repayments, particularly variable rate products, which can significantly reduce your interest cost and loan term. Fixed rate personal loans may have limits on extra repayments or charge early repayment fees (break costs). Finance You will identify which loans allow extra repayments if this feature is important to you.

Common fees on personal loans include: establishment/application fee ($0–$600), ongoing monthly fee ($0–$15/month), and early repayment fee (on some fixed rate products). The comparison rate (which incorporates fees into the rate display) is the most useful way to compare the true cost of different loan products. Finance You will provide you with full disclosure of all fees and a comparison rate for any loan we recommend.

Applying for a personal loan results in a hard credit enquiry, which temporarily reduces your credit score slightly. Multiple applications in a short period can have a more significant negative effect. Making all repayments on time will positively impact your credit score over time. Defaulting or making late repayments will negatively impact it. Finance You works to minimise unnecessary credit enquiries by matching you with suitable lenders before formally applying.
